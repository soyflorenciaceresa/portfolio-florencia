Redesigning a Short-Term Rental Platform

Location

Argentina · Mexico

Year

2026

Role

Web Designer & Frontend Developer

Scope

Web design

Better Together Homes is a hospitality brand with an investment mindset that manages premium short-term rentals across Argentina and Mexico. Since 2019 it has grown to 150 properties in more than ten cities, from Tulum to Bariloche. We refreshed its brand and built its digital platform, from strategy to the last line of code.

View project

The challenge

The website had become outdated and no longer reflected the business's updated brand identity. Its basic booking functionality offered a limited experience for guests, while the platform needed to communicate clearly with three distinct audiences: guests, property owners and investors.

The challenge was to evolve the website into a modern, fully integrated platform that could serve these different audiences, support a more complete user journey and meet the needs of a growing property management business. A key technical hurdle was connecting the booking system to the website, an integration the team had been trying to achieve for over a year.

Solution

We rethought the website from the ground up, starting with information architecture and content strategy to create clear, tailored journeys for three audiences: guests, property owners and investors. We designed the full UI and built a navigable Figma prototype, translating BT Homes' refreshed identity into a modern, premium experience that balances clarity with warmth.

The new website was designed to go beyond basic bookings. Fully responsive and available in English and Spanish, it brings property discovery, destination content and booking functionality together in one cohesive experience.

To bring the design to life, we collaborated closely with a developer and the Hostify team, integrating the booking engine and APIs to connect the website with the property management system behind BT Homes' bookings.

Built with Sanity, a headless CMS, the platform also enables the team to manage properties, destinations and editorial content independently, without touching code. From UX writing and technical SEO to domain configuration, hosting and testing, we worked across the project through to launch.

Impact

The result was a modern, bilingual platform that brings the BT Homes experience together in one place. Fully integrated with its booking system, the website goes beyond property listings to support guests, property owners and investors through clearer, more tailored journeys. Now live at bthomes.world, it provides a scalable foundation for the business to expand into new destinations.
