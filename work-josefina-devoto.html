Website Design for a Photographer

Location

Buenos Aires, Argentina

Year

2025

Role

Web Designer & Developer

Scope

Web design

A photographer and graphic designer born in Argentina and rooted in Buenos Aires. She chases light and shadow and the small, overlooked details that make a place feel unrepeatable. We designed and built a website where her images speak for themselves.

View project

The challenge

Josefina's practice has two sides: commercial photography and filmmaking for brands, and a collection of fine art prints made for people's walls. Each speaks to a different visitor, a client who wants to hire her and a collector who wants to buy. Her site had to serve both without blurring them, and without letting words get in the way of the images.

Solution

We split the site into two clear paths. Portfolio groups her commercial work by discipline (Fashion, Interiors & Retail, Food, Travel and more), so a brand finds relevant work quickly. Fine Art presents her prints as themed collections (Rocks, People, Windows, Water, Green, Food, Lanzarote) with information on how to buy. The design is minimal and photography-led, with menus and text kept quiet. A short About page tells her story in her own voice, and a single contact form invites both kinds of enquiry: let's make something together, or get some art on your walls. The site is built so Josefina can add new series, update her collections and change images on her own.

Impact

A site that works as her studio and her gallery at once: a portfolio to send to brands, a collection of prints to share with collectors and one place where both can reach her, from Buenos Aires to anywhere in the world.
