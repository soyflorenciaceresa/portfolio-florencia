A Digital Portfolio for a Creative Director

Location

Madrid, Spain

Year

2026

Role

Web Designer & Developer

Scope

Web design

A creative director and brand image consultant born in Buenos Aires and based in Madrid. We designed and built a website where her work leads and her eye does the talking. Because she works across fashion, jewelry, gastronomy, hospitality and events, the site had to feel as cinematic as the work it holds.

View project

The challenge

Lucila works across fashion, jewelry, gastronomy, hospitality and events, from creative direction to table styling. Her work was strong, but scattered. She needed one place that could hold all of it, speak to brands in any market and let her keep it updated on her own.

Solution

A portfolio that behaves like an editorial: images come first, text steps back, and every project is presented the way she would present it in person, curated, with space and intention. The design is clean, minimal and high-end, with photography as the protagonist. The work is organized by industry, so a hotel, a jewelry brand or a restaurant can find what speaks to them in seconds, with a clear path from portfolio to services to contact. We also wrote every word on the site: her bio, her services and the way each project is introduced. The site is built so Lucila can add projects, update services and change images herself, without depending on anyone.

Impact

A website that works as her main business tool: a portfolio to show, a presentation to send and a closing piece for new clients across Spain, Europe and Latin America.
